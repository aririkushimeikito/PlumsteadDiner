[ Drink QUESTIONS ]

Does Plumstead Diner have an espresso bar?

Yes — we pull double espressos, Americanos, cappuccinos, lattes, iced lattes, Nutella lattes and cold brew, with vanilla, caramel, hazelnut and pumpkin flavor shots available.

What milkshake flavors do you have?

Classic milkshakes come in vanilla, chocolate, strawberry, and cookies n’ cream.

What are your smoothies made with?

All of our smoothies are made with Greek yogurt, local honey and fresh fruit — choose from Strawberry Banana, Peach Mango, Chocolate Peanut Butter, or Berry Blast.

What desserts do you serve?

Our dessert case changes regularly — ask your server what’s fresh today. You can always add a scoop of ice cream to any dessert for 2.99.
