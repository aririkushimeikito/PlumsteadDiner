[ Menu QUESTIONS ]

Is breakfast really served all day?

Yes — every breakfast item, from omelettes and bennys to pancakes and skillets, is served all day, from 7:00 AM until we close at 3:00 PM, seven days a week.

What wing sauces do you offer?

Pick from mango habanero, BBQ, pub sauce, hot honey, Nashville hot, honey mustard, and garlic parm.

Do you have plant-based or gluten-free options?

Yes — we serve a plant-based Impossible Burger, veggie omelettes, wraps and salads, and gluten-free bun upgrades are available on many items for $1.

Can I make any sandwich a half sandwich with soup or salad?

Yes — any classic handheld sandwich can be turned into a half sandwich served with a bowl of soup or a tossed salad.
