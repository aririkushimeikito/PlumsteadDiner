About Plumstead Diner

A locally owned neighborhood diner in Pipersville, PA — proud to serve Bucks County breakfast and lunch, every single day.

Plumstead Diner is a locally owned neighborhood diner in Pipersville, PA, built around the timeless traditions of a classic American diner, freshly brewed coffee, generous portions, and friendly service that makes you feel right at home from the moment you walk in. Conveniently located at 5978 Easton Road, Pipersville, PA 18947. We’re proud to be a go-to destination for breakfast and lunch in Bucks County.

Our menu features all your diner favorites, from classic egg platters, fluffy pancakes, and omelets made to order, to hearty lunch options like sandwiches, burgers, and daily specials. Everything is made fresh daily using quality ingredients, with a focus on consistency, value, and great taste. Whether you’re stopping in for a quick breakfast, meeting friends for lunch, or enjoying a relaxed meal with family, Plumstead Diner offers a welcoming atmosphere for every occasion.

At Plumstead Diner, we believe great food goes hand in hand with great service. Our team is dedicated to creating a comfortable, friendly environment where guests feel like regulars from their very first visit. With affordable pricing and a menu designed for all ages, we aim to be a reliable, everyday dining spot the whole community can enjoy.

We’re proud to be part of the Pipersville community and look forward to serving our neighbors for years to come. Stop in, grab a seat, and experience a true local diner done right.

[ To us, YOU’RE ONE OF THE FAMILY ]

“Where friends and family can come and unplug from the world, spend time with each other, and break bread together.”

Our mission is to bring our families’ 30 years of experience in the restaurant business to our new and growing community — to serve the community our food and give them a high level of customer service, to give back to the community and help Plumstead grow as we grow, and to maintain a consistency that becomes a standard to our place and makes a comforting environment.

We love our customers and believe that when you get to know us, that will show. From buying local products, to supporting the community, to preparing our food with high standards and high quality ingredients, we promise to always do the best we can for you — inside and outside the kitchen.

You are always welcome at the Plumstead Diner. Thank you for your patronage.

Is Plumstead Diner locally owned?

What kind of food does Plumstead Diner serve?

All your diner favorites — classic egg platters, fluffy pancakes, omelets made to order, plus hearty lunch options like sandwiches, burgers, and daily specials, made fresh daily with quality ingredients.

Is Plumstead Diner family friendly?

Absolutely. With affordable pricing and a menu designed for all ages, we aim to be a reliable, everyday dining spot the whole community can enjoy.
