[ About PLUMSTEAD DINER ]

Plumstead Diner is a locally owned neighborhood diner in Pipersville, PA, built around the timeless traditions of a classic American diner — freshly brewed coffee, generous portions, and friendly service that makes you feel right at home from the moment you walk in. We’re proud to be a go-to destination for breakfast and lunch in Bucks County.

[ Frequently ASKED QUESTIONS ]

What are Plumstead Diner’s hours?

We’re open seven days a week, Monday through Sunday, from 7:00 AM to 3:00 PM.

Where is Plumstead Diner located?

You’ll find us at 5978 Easton Road, Pipersville, PA 18947 — right in the heart of Plumstead Township in Bucks County, Pennsylvania.

Do you serve breakfast all day?

Yes! Every breakfast item on our menu — omelettes, pancakes, bennys, skillets and more — is served all day, every day.

Do you offer a cash discount?

Yes. Menu prices do not reflect a 3% cash discount that is applied at checkout when you pay with cash.

Do you have gluten-free or vegetarian options?

We offer gluten-free bun upgrades on many sandwiches and breakfast items, a plant-based Impossible Burger, veggie omelettes and wraps, salads, and plenty of lighter choices under our Healthy Habits section.
